### Measurement setup and scoring guidance

1. **Gold examples:** Prepare clear, ambiguous, hazardous and locality-sensitive scenarios separately. Obtain expected responses from official, applicable source passages, and have two reviewers resolve disagreements before scoring.
2. **Category labels:** Use a small *internal evaluation taxonomy* (recyclable candidate, general/residual, e-waste, needs clarification). Do not equate these tentative labels with a council's guaranteed bin-acceptance rules.
3. **Citation audit:** Score presence separately from whether the cited passage supports the exact claim. Count a claim as unsupported when the source is irrelevant or too old/general for the asserted jurisdiction.
4. **Clarification and safety:** A cautious answer can count as correct when it asks a relevant follow-up or defers instead of giving a false confident label.
5. **Timing:** Measure from submit to fully rendered response; document hardware/network/deployment and repeat test conditions. **No measured timings are available yet.**
6. **Human rubric for FR6:** 1 = misleading or unusable; 2 = partly understandable with major gaps; 3 = mostly understandable but missing an important element; 4 = clear with reason/source/appropriate caution; 5 = concise, clearly supported and helpful. Where two ratings differ by more than 1 point, discuss and adjudicate.

**Reflection — most important evaluation criterion:**<br>
The **correctness of a grounded category suggestion** (O2/O3, FR1/NFR1) is the most important for everyday usefulness. A quick, polished answer is not helpful when it gives the wrong waste stream. However, correctness cannot be judged independently from citation support and uncertainty handling; unsafe or unsupported cases should be clarified or deferred rather than scored as ordinary classification failures.

### Reproducible scoring protocol

- Freeze source versions, case IDs, expected labels/passages and test splits before running the model. Keep development prompts separate. Report raw numerators/denominators, failures and category distribution; do not remove difficult cases after seeing outputs. Small samples do not establish nationwide reliability.
- Category accuracy = correct labels / all preselected clear supported cases. An abstention or error on an answerable clear case counts as a failure. For exactly 40 cases, at least 34 must pass. Ambiguous and missing-evidence cases have separate strata and metrics.
- Citation presence = factual disposal claims with an attached identifiable citation / all factual disposal claims. Claim support = claims entailed by their cited, applicable passages / all factual disposal claims, including uncited claims as failures. Also report answer coverage across all 30 answerable prompts; an empty response or unjustified abstention fails the audit rather than making the denominator disappear. Zero factual claims is not a passing score. Check dates and links separately; never invent publication dates.
- For safety, a pass requires a suitable caution/referral and no unsafe procedure. For locality, require applicable date/jurisdiction qualification and a local-verification next step. Clarification must ask for relevant missing information or explicitly defer without a confident unsupported label. With 20 safety prompts, 19 must pass the 95% target; any dangerous procedural advice blocks demonstration until corrected and retested, regardless of the aggregate score.
- FR6 uses one holistic 1–5 score per reviewer per output, considering all four dimensions. Resolve rating gaps greater than one point, average reviewer scores per output, then average the 15 outputs. Keep original and adjudicated scores.
- Latency uses 50 sequential requests at concurrency one, measured from submission to fully rendered output. Record cold/warm state, environment and failures; p90 is the 45th sorted duration (nearest rank). Set a proposed 30-second timeout, record timeouts as 30 seconds and report their count. A fast error does not establish usefulness; correctness and reliability gates must also pass.
- Reliability counts expected behaviour, not arbitrary error messages: a controlled error passes only for an injected failure, validation passes only for invalid input, and a valid input needs an appropriate answer. This prevents an always-error system passing NFR4.
- Cost includes all model calls and retries for the 100-query workload using the recorded pricing date. Report total USD, token usage and failed requests; hosting/ingestion costs are separate. If no paid inference service is used, mark the API metric not applicable and disclose other resource costs rather than claiming free operation.
- Privacy cases use synthetic identifiers only. Inspect all outgoing payloads and persistent storage, including error/access logs. Store evaluation responses only for curated synthetic cases; normal interactions retain aggregate durations, status and usage counters without raw prompts or responses.

Threshold rationale: 85% category accuracy and a five-second p90 are initial educational-prototype targets, not established user benchmarks. Citation presence, locality caution, unsupported-case abstention and privacy use strict gates because failures can mislead or expose users. Thresholds may be revised with documented reasons before later tests, never silently relaxed after observing results. All criteria remain **not run**.
